When you purchase or subscribe to a Zena service, we may collect information relating to:

  • Subscription plan
  • Trial or subscription status
  • Transaction or order ID
  • Payment status
  • Subscription start and renewal dates
  • Payment-related information received from our payment service providers

We generally do not directly store complete payment card, banking, or other sensitive payment credentials when these are processed by third-party payment providers.

We may collect information you provide when you:

We may receive information from third parties such as:
